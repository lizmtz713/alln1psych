# Direction Gauge — Final Scientific Definition

**Source of truth for the product and codebase. Do not conflate Direction with productivity or goal completion.**

---

## What Direction Measures

**Direction = clarity and sense of orientation in life.**

It answers:

- **"Do I know where I'm going?"**

It does **not** answer:

- "Did I finish my tasks today?"

Those are different psychological systems. Direction is **navigation**; goals are **tools**.

---

## What Direction Reflects

- Purpose  
- Life focus  
- Decision clarity  
- Sense of progress (orientation, not completion)  
- Future orientation  

**Scientific foundations:** Self-Determination Theory, Meaning & Purpose research, Motivation psychology, Goal orientation theory.

People with high Direction typically report: higher resilience, lower anxiety, stronger motivation.

---

## Direction vs Goals

| Direction | Goals |
|-----------|--------|
| Become a healthier person | Run a marathon |
| Life orientation | Concrete targets |
| **Navigation** | **Tools** |

If a goal fails, Direction can still exist. Separating them is what makes the model powerful.

---

## How Direction Should Update

- Direction should **not** change daily. It moves **slowly**.
- It changes with: life clarity, decision confidence, goal alignment, sense of meaning.
- **Low Direction signals:** "I feel lost", "I don't know what I'm doing with my life".
- **High Direction signals:** "I know what matters right now", "I have a clear focus".

---

## Check-In (In-App)

The Direction check-in question is:

**"How clear does your path feel right now?"**

Scale:

- Very clear  
- Mostly clear  
- Somewhat uncertain  
- Lost  

This feeds the Direction gauge. Do **not** use task- or completion-based questions for Direction.

---

## System Chain

```
Values (Alignment)
    ↓
Direction (clarity of life path)
    ↓
Goals (concrete actions)
    ↓
Daily behavior
    ↓
Reflection
    ↓
Insight
```

**Alignment → Direction → Goals** keeps the system coherent. Goals tie back to Alignment via "Why does this goal matter to you?" in the Goal Builder.

---

## Six-Gauge Structure

- **Top 3 (inner state):** Body, State, Emotion  
- **Bottom 3 (life structure):** Connection, **Direction**, Alignment  

**Direction = life orientation.** Keep copy and logic consistent with this definition everywhere.
