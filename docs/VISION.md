## WHAT THIS IS

AllN1 Psych is NOT a therapy app. It is NOT a meditation app. It is NOT a mood tracker.

It is an **Emotional Intelligence Operating System** — a personal life companion that:
- Teaches you about yourself (brain, body, emotions, psychology)
- Listens without judgment, 24/7
- Speaks to you at YOUR level (age, cognitive development, emotional state)
- Quietly bridges the gap between people who are struggling and people who love them
- Replaces the life manual nobody ever gave you

**The core insight:** Most humans will never do therapy. Not because they don't need it — because they can't afford it, don't trust it, are scared of it, don't know they need it, or tried it and it didn't solve anything. Problem solving starts from yourself, from your home, from your environment. This app meets people there.

## THE FIVE PILLARS

### Pillar 1: AI Companion ("Your Space")
The core interaction. A safe, private space where you talk to AI — by VOICE first, text if preferred.

**How It Works:**
- Open the app → speak freely → AI listens, understands, responds
- AI adapts communication style to your:
  - Age and developmental level (10-year-old gets different language than 40-year-old)
  - Emotional state (if you're angry, AI doesn't lecture — it meets you there first)
  - History (AI remembers every conversation, never triggers carelessly)
  - Love language (words of affirmation, quality time, acts of service, etc.)
  - Communication preferences (direct vs gentle, humor vs serious)
- NOT a chatbot. A companion that knows you deeply over time
- Voice-first: you speak, AI listens and responds with voice. Like talking to a friend
- Text option always available for when you can't speak out loud
- Private journaling mode: vent without needing a response, AI still learns from it

**Key AI Behaviors:**
- Never judges
- Never minimizes ("That's not a big deal" is BANNED)
- Always validates the emotion first, then gently helps process
- Asks permission before giving advice ("Would you like my thoughts, or do you just need me to listen?")
- Remembers: "Last time you felt this way was after your argument with Sarah in March. Is this similar?"
- Adapts vocabulary and concepts to user's age/level
- Uses metaphors and examples appropriate to the user's world
- Celebrates wins, big and small
- Provides daily check-ins personalized to what's going on in your life

### Pillar 2: Emotional Education ("Know Yourself")
The life manual nobody gave you.

**Modules (Adaptive to Age/Level):**

**For Kids (8-12):**
- "Feelings Detective" — learn to identify what you're feeling and why
- "Brain Basics" — why your brain does what it does (age-appropriate neuroscience)
- "Emotion Superpowers" — turning big feelings into strengths
- "Friend Skills" — navigating social situations
- Interactive stories and scenarios (not boring lectures)

**For Teens (13-17):**
- Identity and self-discovery
- Managing social media pressure and comparison
- Understanding hormones and mood swings (without being condescending)
- Healthy relationships vs toxic ones
- Dealing with academic pressure
- Understanding your parents' perspective (and helping them understand yours)
- Digital wellness and screen time awareness

**For Adults (18+):**
- Emotional regulation techniques
- Attachment styles and how they affect relationships
- Communication skills (assertiveness, boundaries, conflict resolution)
- Understanding your triggers and patterns
- Stress management and burnout prevention
- Self-compassion practices
- Parenting emotional intelligence

**For Older Adults (60+):**
- Companionship-focused interactions
- Processing life transitions (retirement, loss, health changes)
- Staying connected and fighting isolation
- Legacy and purpose conversations
- Cognitive engagement activities

**Delivery:** Short daily lessons (5-10 min), interactive exercises, real-world practice challenges. AI references these lessons in conversations when relevant ("Remember what we learned about triggers last week? This sounds like that pattern.")

### Pillar 3: Temperature System ("Your Circle")
The breakthrough feature. Connecting people who love each other without requiring anyone to ask for help.

**How It Works:**

1. **User sets their circle:** Invite 2-5 trusted people (family, friends, anyone)
2. **Temperature is calculated automatically** based on:
   - Mood check-ins (quick daily tap: 😊 😐 😟 😢 or voice "Hey, rough day")
   - Conversation patterns with AI (frequency, emotional tone, keywords)
   - App usage patterns (increased late-night usage, longer sessions)
   - User-reported events (fight with friend, bad grade, work stress)
3. **Temperature is shared as a simple gauge:**
   - 🟢 Green: "Doing well"
   - 🟡 Yellow: "Could use some love"
   - 🟠 Orange: "Having a hard time"
   - 🔴 Red: "Really struggling — reach out"
4. **Circle members receive gentle nudges:**
   - "Your daughter has been in the yellow zone this week. A text or quality time might mean a lot right now."
   - "Your friend has been having a tough few days. Maybe check in?"
   - "Your dad seems a bit isolated lately. A call would brighten his week."
5. **Nudges are NEVER specific:** No details about what's wrong. No conversation content. Just the temperature and a suggestion for how to show up

**Universal Relationships (NOT just parent-child):**
- Parent → Child (any age)
- Child → Aging Parent
- Sibling → Sibling
- Friend → Friend
- Spouse → Spouse
- Mentor → Mentee
- Anyone → Anyone they love

**CRITICAL TRUST MODEL:**
- User CHOOSES to share temperature (opt-in, never automatic)
- User can pause sharing anytime without notifying circle
- User controls WHAT contributes to their temperature
- Circle members NEVER see conversation content
- User can set different sharing levels per person
- Teen/child version: during onboarding, clearly explains what parent can and cannot see
- Transparency is everything: "Your mom can see that you're having a hard week, but she can NEVER see what you've told me"

**The Magic:**
- A teen doesn't have to awkwardly say "Mom, I'm not okay"
- A parent doesn't have to guess what's wrong
- A friend doesn't have to wait until someone calls crying
- An aging parent doesn't have to admit they're lonely
- Love finds its way through without anyone having to be vulnerable first

### Pillar 4: AI Role Play ("Practice Life")
Practice any real-world scenario with AI before doing it for real.

**Use Cases:**
- Practice asking your boss for a raise
- Practice telling a friend you're hurt
- Practice a job interview
- Practice setting a boundary with a family member
- Practice apologizing
- Practice breaking up with someone
- Practice telling your parents something scary
- Practice saying no
- Practice asking for help

**How It Works:**
- Tell AI the scenario: "I need to tell my mom I'm failing math"
- AI plays the other person realistically (can adjust difficulty: supportive, neutral, or challenging)
- After practice, AI debriefs: what went well, what to adjust, how to handle reactions
- User can replay scenarios multiple times with different approaches
- AI helps with specific phrases: "Try saying it like this..."
- Builds confidence through repetition and preparation

### Pillar 5: Daily Companion ("Your Day")
Small daily touchpoints that keep you connected to yourself.

**Features:**
- **Morning Check-in:** "How are you starting today?" (voice or tap)
- **Daily Intention:** AI suggests a focus based on your recent history
- **Encouragement:** Personalized to your love language and situation
  - Words of affirmation: "You handled yesterday really well. I'm proud of you."
  - Acts of service: "Here's something small that might make today easier..."
  - Quality time: "Let's take 5 minutes together right now."
- **Evening Reflection:** "How did today go? Anything on your mind?"
- **Weekly Insights:** "Here's what I noticed about your week..." (patterns, growth, areas of attention)
- **Milestone Celebrations:** "You've been checking in for 30 days straight. That takes real commitment."
- **Gratitude Prompts:** Personalized, not generic

## SAFETY & CRISIS PROTOCOL

**This is non-negotiable and must be built from day one.**

**Tier 1 — Passive Monitoring:**
- AI continuously monitors for concerning language patterns
- Keywords and sentiment analysis running in background
- Escalation scoring based on frequency, intensity, and context

**Tier 2 — Gentle Check-in:**
- If patterns suggest increasing distress, AI asks directly but gently:
  - "I've noticed you've been going through a lot lately. I want to make sure you're safe. Are you having thoughts of hurting yourself?"
- User's response determines next step

**Tier 3 — Resource Offering:**
- If user indicates crisis, AI immediately provides:
  - 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988)
  - Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741)
  - Local emergency resources
  - Option to call 911
- AI stays present: "I'm here with you right now. You are not alone."

**Tier 4 — Circle Alert (with consent):**
- "Would you like me to let someone in your circle know you could use support right now?"
- Only activated with user's explicit permission
- If user is a minor and situation is high-risk, app follows mandatory reporting guidelines (this requires legal review)

**What AI Never Does:**
- Never diagnoses conditions
- Never prescribes medication or treatment
- Never claims to replace professional help
- Never dismisses or minimizes distress
- Never shares conversation content with circle members

## VOICE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

Liz's vision: "We don't type anymore. We just speak."

**Primary Interaction Flow:**
1. Open app → microphone is ready
2. Speak naturally: "Hey, I had a really bad day..."
3. AI processes speech → understands context, emotion, history
4. AI responds with voice (warm, natural, appropriate to user's profile)
5. Conversation flows naturally like talking to a trusted friend
6. Everything is transcribed and stored privately for recall and pattern analysis

**Voice Features:**
- Wake word option: "Hey Psych" (optional, for hands-free)
- Emotion detection from voice tone (not just words)
- Speed and volume adaptation (if user is whispering, AI responds softly)
- Background noise handling
- Multi-language support (future)

**Text Always Available:**
- Late at night when you can't talk out loud
- In public spaces
- When you need to write it out to process
- Preference setting: voice-first, text-first, or mixed

## ONBOARDING FLOW

**First Time (The Most Important 5 Minutes):**

1. **Welcome:** "Welcome to your space. This is just for you. Everything here is private."
2. **Age/Stage:** "How old are you?" (determines communication level and content)
3. **Name & Pronouns:** "What should I call you? And what pronouns do you use?"
4. **Voice or Text:** "Do you prefer to talk or type? You can always switch."
5. **Love Language Quick Assessment:** 5 quick questions to determine primary love language
6. **First Conversation:** AI has a genuine getting-to-know-you chat — not a form, a real conversation
   - "What's something that's been on your mind lately?"
   - "Who are the most important people in your life?"
   - "What does a good day look like for you?"
7. **Circle Setup (Optional):** "Is there someone who cares about you that you'd like to stay connected with through this app? You control everything they see."
8. **Promise:** "Nothing you tell me leaves this space unless you choose to share it. I'm here whenever you need me."

## DATA MODEL (Supabase)

### Core Tables:

**users**
- id, email, name, pronouns, date_of_birth, age_group (auto-calculated)
- love_language, communication_preference (voice/text/mixed)
- onboarding_completed, created_at, updated_at

**conversations**
- id, user_id, started_at, ended_at
- mode (voice/text/mixed), summary (AI-generated)
- emotional_tone (AI-assessed: positive/neutral/negative/crisis)
- topics (AI-extracted tags)

**messages**
- id, conversation_id, role (user/ai), content
- voice_transcript (if voice), emotion_detected
- timestamp

**mood_checkins**
- id, user_id, mood_score (1-5), mood_label
- note (optional), voice_note_url (optional)
- context_tags, timestamp

**temperature**
- id, user_id, current_temp (green/yellow/orange/red)
- calculated_at, contributing_factors (JSON)
- trend (improving/stable/declining)

**circles**
- id, user_id (the person being monitored)
- circle_member_id (the person who receives nudges)
- relationship_type (parent, child, sibling, friend, spouse, mentor, other)
- sharing_level (full_temp, limited, paused)
- accepted, created_at

**circle_nudges**
- id, circle_id, nudge_type
- message (generic, never specific)
- sent_at, read_at, acted_on

**education_progress**
- id, user_id, module_id, lesson_id
- completed, score, reflection_notes
- started_at, completed_at

**role_play_sessions**
- id, user_id, scenario_description
- ai_role, difficulty_level
- transcript, debrief_notes
- created_at

**journal_entries**
- id, user_id, content, voice_transcript
- ai_analysis (patterns, emotions, themes)
- private (always true — never shared)
- created_at

**daily_touchpoints**
- id, user_id, type (morning_checkin, intention, encouragement, evening_reflection)
- content, user_response
- date

## LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS (Must Address Before Launch)

1. **COPPA Compliance** — Users under 13 require parental consent. Must implement verified parental consent flow.
2. **HIPAA** — If collecting health-related data, may need HIPAA compliance. Consult healthcare attorney.
3. **Mandatory Reporting** — If a minor discloses abuse or imminent harm, legal obligations vary by state. Need clear policy.
4. **Data Privacy** — Conversations are deeply personal. Encryption at rest and in transit. Clear privacy policy. User data deletion on request.
5. **AI Disclaimer** — Clear messaging that AI is not a licensed therapist and does not provide medical advice.
6. **Terms of Service** — Age-appropriate TOS. Separate for minors.
7. **Insurance** — Professional liability insurance recommended.
