## 3. App Description (4000 characters max)

**You are not broken. You are a system. And now you have a dashboard.**

Welcome to InGauge — the first Personal Health Operating System for the Mind. Think of it as a cockpit for your inner world: six intuitive gauges that help you understand what's happening inside, and powerful AI tools to help you navigate what comes next.

**THE HUMAN COCKPIT**

Most wellness apps ask "How are you?" and stop there. InGauge goes deeper with six gauges designed by a psychologist and grounded in real science:

• **Body** — Physical sensations and energy levels
• **State** — Your nervous system's current mode (safe, alert, or shutdown)
• **Emotion** — What you're actually feeling, with granularity that matters
• **Connection** — How linked you feel to others right now
• **Direction** — Your sense of purpose and momentum
• **Alignment** — How true you're living to your values

Check in daily. Watch your patterns emerge. Start to understand yourself like never before.

**TALK TO PSYCH**

Your AI companion who actually gets you. Psych remembers your context, understands your patterns, and offers thoughtful perspective — not generic platitudes. It's like having a wise friend available 24/7.

**7 AI-POWERED TOOLS**

• **Relate** — Understand the people in your life on a deeper level
• **Replay** — Process difficult events through 5 guided phases
• **Role Play** — Practice tough conversations before you have them
• **Journal** — Reflect with AI-guided prompts tailored to you
• **Help** — Learn how to support someone you care about
• **Decode** — Analyze confusing messages (screenshot support included)
• **Love** — Navigate relationships, intimacy, and connection

**CIRCLE: KNOW HOW YOUR PEOPLE ARE REALLY DOING**

Share your "temperature" with loved ones and see theirs. No awkward check-ins, no intrusive texts. Just a gentle, visual way to stay connected to what matters most.

**THE HUMAN MANUAL**

48+ science-backed lessons that teach you how *you* work. Understand your nervous system, emotional patterns, attachment style, and more. This isn't pop psychology — it's built on 22+ academic textbooks and real research.

**CRISIS SUPPORT — ALWAYS FREE**

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Trans Lifeline, Trevor Project, and more. One tap away, 24/7/365. Some things should never have a paywall.

**WHY INGAUGE IS DIFFERENT**

We didn't build another meditation timer or mood tracker. We built what should have existed all along: a complete operating system for understanding your inner world. Grounded in Polyvagal Theory, Emotional Granularity research, and the lived experience of being human.

You deserve to understand yourself. Start your journey today.

**Recommended Rating: 12+**

**Justifications:**

| Content Type | Present? | Notes |

| Infrequent/Mild Medical/Treatment Information | Yes | Mental health education, nervous system science, emotional wellness content |
| Infrequent/Mild Mature/Suggestive Themes | Yes | Love tool covers relationships and intimacy in age-appropriate, educational context |
| Unrestricted Web Access | No | All content is in-app |
| Gambling | No | — |
| Horror/Fear Themes | No | — |
| Alcohol/Tobacco/Drug Reference | No | — |
| Sexual Content/Nudity | No | — |
| Profanity/Crude Humor | No | — |
| Violence | No | — |

**Crisis Content Consideration:**
The app includes crisis resources (988, Trans Lifeline, Trevor Project). This is protective content, not triggering content. Apple generally views crisis support favorably.

**AI Chat Consideration:**
AI conversations are moderated and designed for mental wellness. No generation of harmful content. If App Store Review raises concerns, we can document our safety guardrails.

**Recommendation:** Submit as 12+ initially. Be prepared to adjust to 17+ if Review requests, though 12+ is defensible given the educational, supportive nature of all content.
