# Human Skills: Research Map

**Purpose:** Map the key life skills that research (psychology, sociology, education, behavioral science) shows people need but often don’t learn formally—and how the app teaches or supports each one.

**Big idea:** If the app helps people learn and practice these skills in everyday situations, it becomes a **life-skills training system**, not just a productivity or wellness app.

---

## How the app teaches (effective format)

Research and modern attention spans both support:

1. **Short scenario** — one situation
2. **Brief explanation** — why it matters
3. **Practice tool** — try it in the app
4. **Real-world action** — one small step

Example: *“How to apologize effectively” → quick explanation → example message → practice with Tone Check or Role Play.*

## Why these skills matter

Research on well-being and relationship success consistently highlights:

- **Emotional intelligence** — awareness + regulation (skills 1, 2)
- **Communication competence** — clear, respectful expression (3, 5)
- **Self-regulation** — pause, manage stress (2, 9)
- **Empathy** — perspective-taking (8)
- **Problem-solving** — conflict resolution, decisions, reflection (4, 6, 7, 12)

Together they form **social–emotional skills** that many people were never taught explicitly at school or home.

## Teaching format (reminder)

For each skill, the most effective flow is:

1. **Short scenario** — e.g. “Someone interrupted you. What’s the best response?”
2. **Brief explanation** — one principle (e.g. “Interruptions signal disrespect.”)
3. **Practice tool** — Tone Check, Role Play, Conversation Builder, etc.
4. **Real-world action** — one small step (“Try one validating sentence next conflict.”)

Keep lessons **short** (e.g. 20–40 seconds for the idea); use **tools** for practice and **reflection** for integration.
